The article is Part 5 of Inside the Physical AI Factory. It follows the design sequence from product, demand, and production routing through capacity, flow, factory layout, utilities, site selection, permitting, validation, economics, and expansion.

The continuing hypothetical rover moves through three stages: the first 10 prototypes, the first 100 commercial units, and capacity for 1,000 units annually. All layouts, volumes, calculations, space relationships, and economics are teaching frameworks. They do not describe a real factory, quoted project, guaranteed output, legal requirement, or recommended investment.
