## Editorial Scope

The report teaches readers to see a manufacturing plant as a complete production system. It separates two classification questions: what is transformed, and how work flows. It then tours receiving, storage, fabrication, assembly, quality, packaging, shipping, maintenance, utilities, waste, engineering, and production control.

The continuing hypothetical example is a compact autonomous inspection rover. Its production system evolves from 10 prototypes to the first 100 units and then to capacity for 1,000 units per year. No specifications, prices, suppliers, customers, shipment volumes, or performance results are attributed to a real product.
