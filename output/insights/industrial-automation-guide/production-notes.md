## Editorial scope

The report begins with the control loop and explains sensors, actuators, motors, VFDs, servo drives, PLCs, safety controllers, HMI, SCADA, CNC, robot controllers, industrial networks, and the factory software stack. It follows illustrative rover order R1042 from enterprise planning through deterministic physical action and back into the production record.

All rover order details and system examples are teaching frameworks. They do not describe a real factory, quoted project, validated safety design, or guaranteed performance.

## Research boundary

Primary sources include NIST SP 800 Part 82 Revision 3, the NIST control loop definition, IEC 61131 Part 3, ISO 13849 Part 1, IEC 62061, OSHA machine guarding requirements, ISA 95 materials, OPC Foundation materials, and US Department of Energy Better Plants motor guidance.

Protocol organization materials from ODVA, PROFIBUS and PROFINET International, and the EtherCAT Technology Group were used for terminology and representative architecture context. Product and protocol mentions are examples, not endorsements.
