## Editorial scope

The report moves from conventional automation into physical AI. It establishes a maturity continuum, a physical AI decision loop, the data and edge architecture, application readiness, safety and governance boundaries, and a complete buyer and deployment framework.

Applications include machine vision, adaptive robot work, autonomous material movement, predictive maintenance, digital twins, dynamic scheduling, and generative assistance for engineering and operations. The same hypothetical autonomous inspection rover remains the continuing teaching product.

All rover examples, maturity classifications, authority boundaries, and economic figures are illustrative. They do not describe a real factory, validated safety design, supplier quotation, or guaranteed performance.

## Research boundary

Primary sources include the NIST 2026 roadmap for AI and machine learning in smart manufacturing, NIST industrial AI implementation guidance, the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and Generative AI Profile, NIST augmented intelligence and digital twin programs, ISO 10218, ISO 3691 Part 4, ISO 23247, OPC UA architecture materials, and NIST smart manufacturing cybersecurity guidance.

A 2025 peer reviewed systematic review supplies independent context on AI enhanced digital twins and maintenance adoption. World Economic Forum lighthouse materials provide examples of reported transformation, but participating company results are not treated as universal outcomes.
