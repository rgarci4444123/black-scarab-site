## Calculator

Installed US$150,000; arc 4 min/attempt; other cell time 2 min; setup 30 min/50 attempts; 2,000 h/year; availability 80%; first pass yield 98%; demand 10,000 accepted parts. Capacity floor(2,000 × 60 × .8 × .98 / 6.6) = 14,254 parts.

Manual paid time 12 min/accepted part = 2,000 h. Robot operator time is 3 min/attempt plus setup: 10,000/.98 × 3.6/60 = 612.2449 h. Released time 1,387.7551 h. US$35/h × 75% cash realization less US$12,000 running cost = US$24,428.5714 net benefit; payback 6.14035 years. At 100% realization, payback 4.10156 years.

The model caps savings at demand served, counts failed attempts and setup in both relevant clocks, suppresses nonpositive payback, charges added labor in full, validates blanks/ranges/whole parts and handles zero output. It is an annual average purchase model. The reader supplies inspection/repair attendance in operator time and repair expense in running costs. Service contracts need their recurring fees rather than a purchase payback calculation.
