## Reporting record

AMD announced a definitive agreement to acquire World Labs in an all stock transaction valued at about $8.2 billion. The companies expect closing by the end of 2026, subject to regulatory approvals and customary conditions. The report describes the transaction as signed but not closed.

AMD says Fei Fei Li will join as executive vice president and chief scientist and report to Lisa Su after closing. World Labs says Justin Johnson and Ben Mildenhall will continue leading the research team.

World Labs and Fei Fei Li provide the account of the company founding, Atlas, Marble, the World API, the SceniX acquisition, robot simulation results, early market interest, and the reason for moving closer to hardware. Technical leadership and performance statements are attributed as company claims.

AMD Ventures confirms that it invested in the 2026 financing after an earlier Series B investment and that the companies were already collaborating on training and inference optimization for AMD Instinct accelerators.

CNBC and Bloomberg independently confirm the announced transaction value, structure, leadership change, and expected closing period. CNBC provides the comparison with AMD's Xilinx acquisition. Bloomberg independently reported the $1 billion World Labs financing in February.

No public revenue, paid customer count, model usage, gross margin, infrastructure cost, operating loss, cash burn, purchase accounting, expected dilution, retention package, or integration expense was found. Atlas remains in early access, and public SceniX performance evidence comes from World Labs rather than an independent evaluation.
