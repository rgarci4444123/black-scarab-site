# Zipline cover brief

Story thesis: Financing and a major retail expansion move aerial delivery toward a national operating network.
Visual thesis: A short airborne connection turns an ordinary neighborhood into a delivery network.
Medium: Bold digital screen print with engraved line work and dense ink, not paper construction.
Subject and scale: One generic autonomous delivery aircraft and a small suspended pod against a sweeping neighborhood landscape. Conceptual aircraft, not a Zipline product rendering.
Viewpoint: Dramatic upward view from near the descending delivery pod, rooftops low in the frame.
Composition: Strong diagonal from a large foreground pod at lower left to a small aircraft high at right; clean tether; neighborhood sweeps to the horizon.
Palette: Burnt orange sky, electric azure shadows and midnight blue architecture, pale warm highlights.
Lighting: Graphic sunset backlighting.
Texture: Printed ink and fine engraved hatching.
Crop: Main pod, tether and aircraft inside central 75 percent; generous margins; landscape 16:9.
