## Cover brief

Story thesis: Extend Robotics is asking industrial customers to buy completed work while the provider absorbs more of the deployment and performance risk.

Visual thesis: The finished output is visible, while the person, controller, and robot appear as parts of the delivery mechanism.

Medium: Documentary still life photography with restrained editorial staging.

Primary subject and scale: A gloved hand on an unbranded controller and a generic robot gripper arranging completed metal components, shown at hand and end effector scale.

Viewpoint: Elevated oblique close view.

Composition: Asymmetric diagonal split between the operator zone and output zone, with the central action protected for a tighter card crop.

Palette family: Warm sodium amber highlights, slate violet shadows, pale cream, and one restrained red cable.

Lighting: Hard practical workshop flash with crisp shadows.

Surface and texture: Worn rubber, brushed aluminum, scuffed composite, powder coated steel, and fine workshop dust.

Crop plan: The operator hand, controller, gripper, and first completed components remain visible in the wide hero. A central card crop retains the controller to gripper relationship and several finished parts.

Recent patterns deliberately avoided: The six most recent covers use paper sculpture with translucent layers, a bright exploded robot arm collage, an overhead factory model, polished humanoids in wide factory interiors, and a robot lineup. This cover avoids paper construction, exploded assemblies, miniature architecture, humanoid bodies, wide facilities, glowing network lines, and centered robots.

Elements that must not appear: Text, letters, numbers, logos, company marks, watermarks, branded headsets, recognizable Extend Robotics hardware, actual customer deployments, apples, humanoids, science fiction interfaces, or invented screens.
