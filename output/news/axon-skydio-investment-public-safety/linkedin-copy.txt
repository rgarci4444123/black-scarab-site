NEWS | Axon and Skydio

The drone is only the start of the public safety business.

Axon announced roughly $1.2 billion of additional investment in Skydio, alongside exclusive public safety distribution rights in the United States and Canada.

The initial $254.9 million primary purchase closed on November 5. The expected stake of about 37% depends on the remaining transactions, scheduled for late 2026 and early 2027.

Skydio builds autonomous drones for field operations. Axon connects their deployment to live operations and evidence systems. The partnership puts the aircraft, customer relationship and information workflow closer together.

Our report follows the money, the sales agreement and the deployment milestones that will show whether the strategy is working.
