## Direct Google observations

Queries: [mechanical](https://www.google.com/search?q=robotics+mechanical+engineer+career&hl=en&gl=us), [learning](https://www.google.com/search?q=robot+learning+engineer+career&hl=en&gl=us), [simulation](https://www.google.com/search?q=robotics+simulation+engineer+career&hl=en&gl=us), [integration](https://www.google.com/search?q=robotics+integration+engineer+career&hl=en&gl=us). Full observed AX text and four autocomplete captures are retained here.

Mechanical results mixed vacancies, career explanations and first-person transition discussions. Learning related searches exposed salary and requirements; PAA asked broader robotics entry questions. Simulation was dominated by vacancies; junior/entry-level variants appeared in related searches. Integration surfaced employer listings and RoboticsCareer.org; no relevant PAA question was captured. Autocomplete showed salary for all four and the ML/RL, simulation-software and system-integration aliases recorded in the JSON. Remote/job/brand-specific suggestions were observed but do not justify job-board content. Extreme-salary and replacement-by-AI PAA questions do not inform these guides.

## Resource comparison

Pages below were actually opened through web research on November 3. Search snippets alone are not treated as full-page audits. These are qualitative coverage observations, not ranking/authority/difficulty judgments.

| Resource | Useful coverage | Opportunity for Black Scarab |
|---|---|---|
| [UK National Careers Service: Robotics engineer](https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/job-profiles/robotics-engineer) | Clear university, college, apprenticeship and work routes; skills and work settings. UK scope is explicit. | A broad occupation page cannot resolve each specialty's artifacts, technical interfaces or US employer requirements. Keep country-specific pathways separate. |
| [Coursera: Robotics engineering](https://www.coursera.org/articles/robotics-engineering) | Broad introduction, educational options and linked salary references. | Specialty boundaries, source-specific qualifications and tested portfolio decisions need more depth. Its broad mechanical growth benchmark should not become a forecast for each robotics specialty. |
| [The Construct: Robotics Developer](https://www.theconstruct.ai/robotics-developer/) | Concrete ROS, simulation and robotics projects; practical software curriculum. | This is a course landing page. Provide independent career comparisons and a bounded project without treating enrollment or completion as hiring evidence. |
| [Siemens Xcelerator Academy: Robotics Simulation Engineer](https://training.plm.automation.siemens.com/ilt/tracks/detail.cfm?trackID=76) | Industrial path development, event simulation and offline programming. | Explain that this is one industrial simulation pathway; learned-policy dynamics and autonomy simulation are different specialties. |
| [WorldSkills: Robot Systems Integration](https://worldskills.org/what/projects/wsos/2024/events/579/skills/1657/) | Detailed occupational outputs, peripheral equipment, programming, documentation and safety responsibility. | Add practical modern application/software interfaces and verified employer examples. Avoid generalizing its vocational standard into every engineer's credential requirement. |
| [Making Ohio: Automation & Robotics Pathway](https://www.makingohio.com/careers/career-pathways/automation-robotics-pathway/) | Technician-to-integrator progression and competency distinctions. | Explain employer-specific equivalent-experience clauses and deployment deliverables. Do not reuse its wage figures outside their scope. |
| [NODE Robotics: Simulation Engineer](https://node-robotics-gmbh.jobs.personio.com/job/2693092) | Primary employer terminology; robot models, simulated sensors, CI, ROS and simulator choices. | Vacancy content describes one organization, not a general entry guide. This search-time example is terminology evidence, not an addition to November 2 counts. |

The Dexmate Ashby page opened only a JavaScript shell; search snippets showed terminology, but no claim of complete live-page verification is made. Competitor pages are not copied into guide prose or added as filler reading links.

No reliable keyword volume, difficulty, Search Console, traffic or ranking history was available. Consequently these decisions optimize clarity and observed intent; they do not claim a more popular keyword or a forecast. Employer counts remain a separate research asset.
