# Black Scarab News Editorial Standard

This standard applies to News reports only. Insights deep dives keep their existing analytical structure and depth.

## Editorial voice

Write with the clarity and pace of a strong business newsletter while keeping the reporting distinctly Black Scarab. The voice should be informed, conversational, concise, and skeptical without becoming cynical.

Use original language. Do not copy phrases, jokes, headings, or sentence structures from another publication or imitate an individual writer.

Do not use dashes in published copy. Rewrite the sentence or use commas, colons, or periods.

## Story structure

1. Open with the news in one direct sentence.
2. Give the essential numbers, participants, and timing immediately.
3. Explain the company and technology in plain language.
4. Use short, descriptive sections that readers can scan quickly.
5. Include the commercial context and the reason the development matters without explicitly announcing why it is physical AI.
6. Separate company claims from independently reported facts.
7. Add a reality check covering unknowns, limitations, or evidence still needed.
8. End with the concrete signals readers should watch next.

## Formatting and length

Target a five to ten minute read.

Keep most paragraphs between one and three sentences. Use the At a glance box for the three most important facts. Prefer concrete numbers and examples over general statements.

Use approachable section headings such as What happened, Under the hood, Why investors care, Reality check, and What comes next. Vary them by story so the format does not become mechanical.

Humor should be light, relevant, and rare. Never let a joke weaken accuracy or make a serious subject feel trivial.

## Reporting rules

Start with primary sources, including company releases, product documentation, regulatory filings, government records, and research papers. Add reputable independent reporting for context and verification.

Attribute performance figures and forecasts. State clearly when pricing, revenue, customer names, shipment volume, or technical specifications are not public.

Credit Rodolfo Garcia Calderoni, CFA, as the author. Use the same author portrait in the byline and author card.

Create an original, logo free editorial cover in the Black Scarab palette. The image must work as both a wide article hero and a cropped news card.

Before publishing, verify the canonical URL, NewsArticle structured data, social image metadata, main sitemap, news sitemap, RSS feed, build output, live article, and IndexNow submission.
